# Automotive Service Churn Early Warning

An R-based early-warning framework for automotive after-sales churn, with
behavioral feature engineering, leakage-resistant validation, prospective
scoring, and model reliability monitoring.

> This repository is a sanitized portfolio edition. It contains synthetic data
> only. Customer records, vehicle identifiers, internal locations, database
> details, fitted production models, and operational risk lists are excluded.

## Why This Project Matters

This project goes beyond churn classification by building a governance
framework that identifies not only high-risk customers, but also when a model
should not be trusted.

The research distinguishes a genuine early-warning problem from apparent churn
signals caused by list-expiration rules or customers who have already completed
their move elsewhere. The final design predicts whether a currently active
vehicle will still be absent three months later.

## Anonymized Study Highlights

- The frozen training sample covered **419,469 vehicle-month observations** and
  **256 positive events** from June 2025 through March 2026, with label
  observation through June 2026.
- The first frozen prospective cohort contained **42,895 eligible vehicles**;
  the operational review capacity was fixed at the **top 5% (2,145 scores)**.
- When labels matured, the cohort contained **44 actual events**. The Champion
  achieved **1.82x Top-5% lift** and **9.09% capture**, compared with **0.91x**
  and **4.55%** for the static Baseline.
- Retrospective paired bootstrap comparisons favored the Champion over the
  static Baseline in **95.2%** of resamples for PR AUC and Top-5% lift, and
  **95.0%** for Top-5% capture. These are win proportions, not accuracy or a
  probability of model superiority; all three 95% confidence intervals crossed
  zero.
- A first-generation model produced an **11.1× concentration anomaly** in a
  small segment. Removing the unstable segment effect reduced the maximum
  concentration ratio to **1.06×**.
- The final Champion passed its shadow reliability gate with score
  **PSI 0.00326** and a **1.02× P99 score ratio**.

These are anonymized aggregate research results, not results generated by the
synthetic demo.

## Evidence by Validation Stage

### Retrospective rolling validation

Paired stratified bootstrap used 500 resamples across three validation months.

| Champion minus static Baseline | Mean difference | 95% CI | Win proportion |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| PR AUC | +0.00170 | [-0.0000789, 0.00665] | 95.2% |
| Top-5% lift | +1.10 | [-0.226, 2.51] | 95.2% |
| Top-5% capture | +0.0553 | [-0.0113, 0.126] | 95.0% |

The retrospective evidence generally favored the behavior model, but it did
not establish statistical significance because every confidence interval
included zero.

### First mature frozen prospective cohort

The June 2026 cohort was frozen before outcomes were available. Its September
2026 outcomes were accepted on October 12, 2026 without changing scores,
membership, target, or model specification.

| Model | ROC AUC | PR AUC | PR AUC / prevalence | Top-5% hits | Top-5% lift | Capture |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Champion | 0.561 | 0.00131 | 1.28 | 4 | 1.82 | 9.09% |
| Challenger | 0.516 | 0.00107 | 1.05 | 1 | 0.454 | 2.27% |
| Baseline | 0.494 | 0.00100 | 0.979 | 2 | 0.909 | 4.55% |

The Champion showed directional ranking value and captured twice as many events
as the Baseline in the review cohort. However, only 44 events matured, a single
hit materially changes lift and recall, discrimination remains modest, and no
prospective bootstrap confidence interval was constructed. This is a first
prospective checkpoint, not production validation.

The mean predicted probability was approximately **0.594x** the observed
prevalence, indicating absolute-risk underprediction. The current model is a
ranking tool and is not a calibrated churn probability.

### Second observation cohort

A September 2026 cohort was created on October 13, 2026. A data-quality gate
blocked scoring after detecting a complete service-day ingestion gap. After
backfill and revalidation, the cohort was sealed with **43,565 eligible raw
records**, **43,548 scored records**, **17 exclusions for missing vehicle age**,
and **2,178 Champion Top-5% scores**.

All three models passed score-reliability checks. Champion monitoring recorded
**PSI 0.00616**, **P99 ratio 1.03**, **maximum segment concentration 1.19x**,
and **maximum within-segment selection rate 5.97%**. Predictive performance
remains pending until labels mature in December 2026; reliability PASS must not
be interpreted as predictive-performance PASS.

## Modeling Design

The frozen `pure_early_warning_v1.1` specification uses:

- **Population:** vehicles still active in the service network, with fewer than
  600 days since the last service and a qualifying within-network relationship.
- **Target:** absent in the third month after the scoring month.
- **Behavior window:** the three months ending at the scoring month.
- **Validation:** purged rolling time splits where every training label matures
  before the validation month.
- **Ranking metrics:** PR AUC, top-5% lift, and top-5% capture rate.
- **Champion:** static vehicle attributes plus six recent service-behavior
  features.
- **Challenger:** static attributes plus recent body-repair count.
- **Baseline:** static attributes only.
- **Governance:** immutable model specification, prospective scoring registry,
  PSI, tail-score drift, and segment-concentration gates.

Location is retained only for monitoring. It is intentionally excluded from all
v1.1 model feature sets.

## Requirements

- R 4.2 or newer
- `testthat` only when running the test suite

The demo pipeline otherwise uses base R so the methodology can be inspected and
run without a large dependency stack.

## Run the Synthetic Demo

From the repository root:

```r
source("scripts/01_run_demo.R", encoding = "UTF-8")
```

The demo:

1. Generates a synthetic monthly vehicle panel.
2. Builds the three-month target using future presence records.
3. Applies the frozen population rules.
4. Runs purged rolling backtests for all three model roles.
5. Fits the Champion on mature labels.
6. Scores a synthetic prospective cohort.
7. Applies the reliability gate.

Generated CSV, RDS, and report outputs are ignored by Git.

## Run Tests

```r
source("scripts/02_run_tests.R", encoding = "UTF-8")
```

Tests protect the target horizon, purge boundary, top-fraction behavior, and
reliability thresholds.

## Interpretation Limits

- Positive events are extremely rare, and the original rolling validation
  covered only three historical validation months.
- Only one frozen prospective cohort has mature outcomes; the promotion policy
  requires at least three mature prospective months.
- Observed behavioral associations are predictive signals, not causal effects.
- A score ranks risk; it does not prove that outreach will recover a customer.
- Intervention effectiveness still requires a treatment-control experiment.
- The model is a research candidate: it is not production-ready, validated for
  intervention, or calibrated as an absolute churn probability.

See the [model card](docs/model_card_v1_1.md) for intended use, exclusions,
validation requirements, and monitoring rules.

## Privacy and Security

T0 data never enters Git. This applies equally to public and private
repositories, branches, tags, pull requests, releases, Actions artifacts, Git
LFS, and encrypted archives.

Do not commit raw or derived customer and vehicle data, identifiers, names,
addresses, phone numbers, plates, chassis identifiers, work orders, service
history, database extracts, logs, screenshots, fitted models, preprocessing
objects, caches, scoring registries, prospective lists, or exported reports.
Public examples must be written from scratch as synthetic data and must never be
created by sampling, masking, or perturbing real records.
